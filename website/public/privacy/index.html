Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-27

The short version

The PubConv app works entirely on your computer. It doesn't upload your files, doesn't need an internet connection and doesn't collect any information about you or your documents.

The PubConv app

  • Your .pub files and the PDFs created from them stay on your computer.
  • The app sends no usage data, crash reports or analytics.
  • It only connects to the internet if you click a link in it (for example "Get the full version"), which opens your web browser.

This website

  • The site uses no tracking cookies and no advertising or analytics scripts.
  • Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and reliability, for a limited time.

Buying and downloads

Purchases and downloads are handled by our payment provider (and by Microsoft if you use the Microsoft Store). They collect what's needed to process your order, such as your name, email address and payment details, under their own privacy policies. We receive your name, email address and order details so we can provide your licence and support. We never see your card details.

Email

If you email us, we use your message only to reply and help you. If you send us a file to investigate a conversion problem, we use it only for that and delete it afterwards.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing support@example.com.
