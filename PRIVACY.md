# PubConv privacy policy

PubConv converts Publisher (.pub) files to PDF entirely on your own computer.

- It collects **no personal data** and sends **nothing** over the internet. Your files never leave your PC.
- It has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, ads or tracking.
- It saves only its own settings (where to save PDFs) on your PC.
- The **Help** and **Donate** buttons open this project's GitHub page in your web browser. What happens there is covered by GitHub's and PayPal's own privacy policies.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/rakawe86/pubconv/issues.
